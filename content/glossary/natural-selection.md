<div class="term-original" lang="en" dir="ltr" markdown="1">
Differential survival or reproduction of different genotypes in a population leading to changes in the gene frequencies of a population. The conditions required for the operation of evolution by natural selection include [variation]({filename}variation.md), a system of [heredity]({filename}heredity.md), [differential reproduction]({filename}differential-reproductive-success.md), and time. For a more detailed explanation, [see our resource on natural selection](https://evolution.berkeley.edu/evolution-101/mechanisms-the-processes-of-evolution/natural-selection/) in Evolution 101.
</div>
