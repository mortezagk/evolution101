<div class="term-original" lang="en" dir="ltr" markdown="1">
Any member of the animal clade Vertebrata. All vertebrates have a backbone that surrounds and protects the nerve cord, a character that they all inherited from their [common ancestor](https://evolution.berkeley.edu/common-ancestor/). Vertebrates are a subgroup of the [chordates]({filename}chordates.md). Modern vertebrates include fish, sharks, mammals, and amphibians.
