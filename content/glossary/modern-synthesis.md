<div class="term-original" lang="en" dir="ltr" markdown="1">
A conception of the processes and mechanisms of evolution that builds on Darwin’s original ideas about evolution by incorporating advances in our understanding of [Mendelian genetics]({filename}mendelian-genetics.md), [molecular genetics]({filename}molecular-genetics.md), and [population genetics]({filename}population-genetics.md). The modern synthesis was developed in the 1930s and 40s and integrates the work of Charles Darwin, Theodosius Dobzhansky, Ronald Fisher, Sewall Wright, and others. For more details, [see our historical essay on the topic](https://evolution.berkeley.edu/the-history-of-evolutionary-thought/1900-to-present/starting-the-modern-synthesis-theodosius-dobzhansky/).
</div>
