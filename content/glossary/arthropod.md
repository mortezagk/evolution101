<div class="term-original" lang="en" dir="ltr" markdown="1">
Any member of the large animal clade, Arthropoda. Living [lineages]({filename}lineage.md) include crustaceans, arachnids, centipedes, millipedes, and insects. [Fossil]({filename}fossil.md) lineages include the extinct [trilobites]({filename}trilobite.md). All arthropods have a hard [exoskeleton]({filename}exoskeleton.md) that is periodically shed during growth, a body that is divided into segments, and jointed legs. These traits were inherited from the [common ancestor]({filename}common-ancestor.md) of all arthropods.
</div>
