<div class="term-original" lang="en" dir="ltr" markdown="1">
Fossils or organisms that show the transformation from an ancestral form to descendant species’ form. For example, there is a well-documented fossil record of transitional forms for the evolution of whales from their amphibious ancestor. For a more detailed explanation, see our [resource on transitional forms](https://evolution.berkeley.edu/lines-of-evidence/transitional-features/).
</div>
